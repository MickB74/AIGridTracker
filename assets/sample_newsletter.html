Negotiation wins

Win
Groton, CT

Vantage signs $2.5M community benefit agreement after year-long moratorium

Groton’s moratorium led to permanent zoning controls — data centers restricted to industrial zones with 45 dBA noise limits at property lines. Vantage agreed to fund a community recreation center and mandatory stormwater management.

Win
The Dalles, OR

Google funds $29M water infrastructure upgrade after community pressure

Residents pushed back on Google’s water draws. The result: Google funded a wastewater treatment upgrade and committed to recycled cooling water. The city capped total data center water use at 25% of municipal supply.

New moratoriums

Enacted
New York — Statewide

Governor signs EO 62: one-year moratorium on facilities over 50 MW

New York becomes the first state with a blanket moratorium on large data centers. EO 62 pauses permitting for 50+ MW facilities while the state studies grid capacity and environmental impact.

New
Seattle, WA

City council proposes pause on new data center permits

Seattle joins the growing list of cities hitting the brakes. The proposal would freeze new permits while the city evaluates power grid strain and water use — a response to three campus applications filed this spring.
