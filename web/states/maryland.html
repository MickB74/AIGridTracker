Illustrative rather than exhaustive — dozens of localities churn weekly, and this tracks the ones we have read a source for. **Each row carries its own provenance.** Rows showing a verification date link to the ordinance, the enacting body's own page, or a datable report of the vote, and were read on that date. Rows marked *unverified* came from bulk compilation and have not been checked against a primary source — the locality, the year, and the status may all be off. Verify one of those with the town clerk before citing it as precedent. **Expiry is derived, not asserted.** A row with a documented end date flips to *Expired* on its own once that date passes. A time-limited moratorium whose start date was never documented keeps showing as enacted — that is a known gap, not a claim that it is still in force. Moratoriums are also routinely extended, so an expiry date is the earliest it could have ended, not proof that it did.

4What Maryland's own study found

Critical Infrastructure Streamlining Act of 2024 & State Data Center Impact Mandate (2024)
Maryland General Assembly (CISA / SB 116)

Legislative response to utility generator permit denials and creation of state-wide environmental impact study.

Passed (May 2024)Streamlining Bill (CISA)
\$30 BillionCanceled Project Investment
168 Diesel GeneratorsControversial Hardware
Sept 1, 2026Comprehensive Study Due
  • Backup Generator Controversy: Aligned Data Centers canceled a massive $30B project in late 2023 after the Maryland PSC denied a permit for 168 diesel backup generators due to air quality emissions thresholds.
  • Regulatory Streamlining (CISA): In May 2024, Maryland enacted the Critical Infrastructure Streamlining Act to exempt data center backup power from Certificate of Public Convenience and Necessity (CPCN) reviews to attract investment.
  • Environmental Safeguards: Environmental groups expressed concern over local particulate and carbon emissions from diesel backup arrays, prompting the state to commission a comprehensive data center impact study due September 1, 2026.

6Where the Maryland paper trail lives

Air & environmental permits: Maryland Dept. of the Environment

No searchable register. Maryland does not publish a permit database you can query. A public-records request to the agency is the only route to the file.

Grid interconnection queue: PJM Interconnection (Mid-Atlantic, Ohio Valley)

A routing hint only — several states are split between markets, and the serving utility is the only authority on which queue a given site is in.
