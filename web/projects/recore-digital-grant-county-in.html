Project dossier · Grant County, Indiana

Recore Digital AI data center inquiry Rumored

An inquiry, not an application. Recore Digital approached the Grant County Area Plan Commission in July 2026 about converting an existing cryptocurrency mining operation in the county into what it describes as a 'quiet, permanent AI Data Center'. The county's 24-month moratorium (Ordinance 4-2026) bars improvement-location permits for data centers in unincorporated areas until March 2028 or until the APC's data center ordinance is adopted, so the live question is what that ordinance will require; a seven-member subcommittee (four APC members, three residents) finished its first review of the draft in August 2026. Site, size, power and water figures have not been published. Coordinates are the county centroid.

State environmental permits

County & town filings

The rezoning application, site plan, traffic and noise studies, staff report and every revision.

  • Grant County Board of Commissioners agendas & packetsThe meeting packet carries the staff report, site plan and conditions — verified page for this locality.
  • Search Grant County planning filingssearchPlanning departments post application files under many different names. Find the department, then ask the clerk for the application number — that number unlocks everything else.
  • Search Grant County property records / GISsearchThe parcel record names the buying LLC and the sale date, which is what ties an anonymous filing to an operator.

Utility & grid records

The electricity request is filed with the grid operator and the utility long before the town hears about it — and states the real load.

  • MISO (Midwest & Gulf) — interconnection queueRequested megawatts, queue position and target in-service date. The MW here is the real number; the one at the town meeting is often phase one.
  • PJM Interconnection (Mid-Atlantic, Ohio Valley) — interconnection queueRequested megawatts, queue position and target in-service date. The MW here is the real number; the one at the town meeting is often phase one.
  • Indiana Utility Regulatory Commission — docket searchSpecial contracts, large-load tariffs and rate cases are docketed here. Testimony and exhibits are public, and are usually more candid than the press release.
  • FERC eLibraryFederal filings: transmission agreements, interconnection service agreements, and co-location disputes.
  • Search the serving utility for a will-serve lettersearchThe will-serve or load-service letter is the utility's own written commitment. Ask the utility and the PUC for it by name — it is frequently the first document to exist.

Federal databases

Works anywhere, and covers the operator's record at its other sites.

  • EPA ECHO facility searchEvery federally-reported air, water and waste permit at a facility, plus its inspection and violation history. Search by address or draw a radius around the site. This project's locality centres on 40.510, -85.660 — search that point with a 5-mile radius.
  • EPA EnvirofactsThe same underlying records, queryable by county — useful for 'what else is permitted near this site' rather than one facility.
  • EPA Facility Registry ServiceResolves the shell LLC on a permit to the facility it belongs to. Worth a look when the filing name means nothing locally.
