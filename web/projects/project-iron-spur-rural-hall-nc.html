What has happened so far (7 events)

  • 2026-03-17announcement
    Charlotte-based developer Drox Group LLC presented its proposed hyperscale data center on roughly 129 acres in Rural Hall at a March community meeting, where residents raised concerns about noise, pollution, and energy and water demands. · source
  • 2026-04-13vote
    The Rural Hall Town Council unanimously passed a resolution in April opposing the rezoning needed for the Drox Group data center. · source
  • 2026-06-11vote
    The Forsyth County (City-County) Planning Board voted on Thursday, June 11 to recommend denying Drox Group's rezoning request for the $3 billion, 129-acre project, with staff citing potential significant offsite impacts to nearby residents. · source
  • 2026-06-23vote
    The Rural Hall Town Council voted to withdraw its opposition and back the project after Mayor Terry Bennett said the developer made a written promise that, if the county approved rezoning, the town could annex the site and collect tax revenue; the resolution carries no legal weight in the county process. · source
  • 2026-07-28news
    Forsyth County Planning Board recommended denial, citing scale and intensity that 'could result in significant offsite impacts to nearby residents.' Rural Hall town officials reversed their opposition after a tax-revenue annexation offer. · source
  • 2026-07-30meeting
    County commissioners held the public hearing on the rezoning after months of resident opposition; a final decision was pending as of this record. · source
  • 2026-07-30vote
    The Forsyth County Board of Commissioners voted 5-2 to deny the rezoning request for Project Iron Spur, with only Don Martin and Gloria Whisenhunt voting in support, following six months of review, over 400 emails, and dozens of public speakers. · source

State environmental permits

Backup generators, water withdrawal and stormwater are permitted by the state, usually earlier and in more detail than anything filed locally.

  • North Carolina DEQ — Division of Air QualityThe program page: which permits this state requires, and the office that issues them.
  • Search North Carolina DEQ — Division of Air Quality for this projectsearchThis state publishes no searchable permit database, so a written records request to the agency is the reliable route. Use this search only to find the right office.

County & town filings

The rezoning application, site plan, traffic and noise studies, staff report and every revision.

  • Search Rural Hall (Forsyth County) planning filingssearchPlanning departments post application files under many different names. Find the department, then ask the clerk for the application number — that number unlocks everything else.
  • Search Rural Hall (Forsyth County) property records / GISsearchThe parcel record names the buying LLC and the sale date, which is what ties an anonymous filing to an operator.

Utility & grid records

The electricity request is filed with the grid operator and the utility long before the town hears about it — and states the real load.

  • PJM Interconnection (Mid-Atlantic, Ohio Valley) — interconnection queueRequested megawatts, queue position and target in-service date. The MW here is the real number; the one at the town meeting is often phase one.
  • North Carolina Utilities Commission — docket searchSpecial contracts, large-load tariffs and rate cases are docketed here. Testimony and exhibits are public, and are usually more candid than the press release.
  • FERC eLibraryFederal filings: transmission agreements, interconnection service agreements, and co-location disputes.
  • Search the serving utility for a will-serve lettersearchThe will-serve or load-service letter is the utility's own written commitment. Ask the utility and the PUC for it by name — it is frequently the first document to exist.

Federal databases

Works anywhere, and covers the operator's record at its other sites.

  • EPA ECHO facility searchEvery federally-reported air, water and waste permit at a facility, plus its inspection and violation history. Search by address or draw a radius around the site. This project's locality centres on 36.240, -80.290 — search that point with a 5-mile radius.
  • EPA EnvirofactsThe same underlying records, queryable by county — useful for 'what else is permitted near this site' rather than one facility.
  • EPA Facility Registry ServiceResolves the shell LLC on a permit to the facility it belongs to. Worth a look when the filing name means nothing locally.

These are places to look, not evidence that anything has been filed. Links were checked on 2026-08-21; search links are queries, not records. What to ask for.
