Project dossier · Andover Township (Sussex County), New Jersey

Andover HPC Data Center In review

National Land Developers registered Andover HPC Development in December 2025 at 248 Stickles Pond Road, a 97-acre former airport site. Andover Township first allowed data centers in a redevelopment zone along Route 206 (September 2025) and raised the height limit to 65 feet (April 2026), but after a chaotic May 2026 meeting with 300 residents, the township passed Ordinance 2026-13 banning data centers township-wide. No formal site plan was ever filed. National Land Developers sued in Sussex County Superior Court on July 10, 2026, challenging the ban.
