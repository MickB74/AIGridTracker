What has happened so far (5 events)

  • 2026-02-02filing
    An OpenAI infrastructure official circulated a mutual non-disclosure agreement to Effingham County officials, asking them to sign before receiving confidential briefings on the project — the start of negotiations conducted outside public view. · source
  • 2026-07-21incentive
    The Effingham County Industrial Development Authority signed a memorandum of understanding with Octans GA, LLC, an OpenAI affiliate, granting a 50% property tax abatement for 15 years locked to the 2026 millage rate. · source
  • 2026-07-22announcement
    County leaders publicly announced the $20 billion, 1,400-acre campus one day after the tax agreement was signed, projecting up to 1,000 permanent jobs, an $80 million community investment fund, and revenue that could underwrite the county's plan to eliminate homestead property taxes. · source
  • 2026-07-24utility
    Reporting confirmed OpenAI had contracted 3.2 gigawatts from Georgia Power for the site, delivered over roughly four years, despite public backlash over the secrecy of the deal. · source
  • 2026-08-08coverage
    An open-records investigation reconstructed how the deal moved from NDA to signed abatement without a public hearing, with residents learning the buyer's identity only after the incentives were locked in. · source

Find the paper trail (0 filed · 11 places to look)

State environmental permits

Backup generators, water withdrawal and stormwater are permitted by the state, usually earlier and in more detail than anything filed locally.

  • Georgia EPD — Air Protection BranchThe program page: which permits this state requires, and the office that issues them.
  • Search Georgia EPD — Air Protection Branch for this projectsearchThis state publishes no searchable permit database, so a written records request to the agency is the reliable route. Use this search only to find the right office.

County & town filings

The rezoning application, site plan, traffic and noise studies, staff report and every revision.

  • Search Rincon (Effingham County) planning filingssearchPlanning departments post application files under many different names. Find the department, then ask the clerk for the application number — that number unlocks everything else.
  • Search Rincon (Effingham County) property records / GISsearchThe parcel record names the buying LLC and the sale date, which is what ties an anonymous filing to an operator.

Utility & grid records

The electricity request is filed with the grid operator and the utility long before the town hears about it — and states the real load.

  • No organised market covers this statenoteThere is no public queue to read. The record is the utility's integrated resource plan and its filings at the PUC — plus FERC eLibrary for anything federally docketed.
  • Georgia Public Service Commission — docket searchSpecial contracts, large-load tariffs and rate cases are docketed here. Testimony and exhibits are public, and are usually more candid than the press release.
  • FERC eLibraryFederal filings: transmission agreements, interconnection service agreements, and co-location disputes.
  • Search the serving utility for a will-serve lettersearchThe will-serve or load-service letter is the utility's own written commitment. Ask the utility and the PUC for it by name — it is frequently the first document to exist.

Federal databases

Works anywhere, and covers the operator's record at its other sites.

  • EPA ECHO facility searchEvery federally-reported air, water and waste permit at a facility, plus its inspection and violation history. Search by address or draw a radius around the site. This project's locality centres on 32.290, -81.240 — search that point with a 5-mile radius.
  • EPA EnvirofactsThe same underlying records, queryable by county — useful for 'what else is permitted near this site' rather than one facility.
  • EPA Facility Registry ServiceResolves the shell LLC on a permit to the facility it belongs to. Worth a look when the filing name means nothing locally.

These are places to look, not evidence that anything has been filed. Links were checked on 2026-08-21; search links are queries, not records. What to ask for.
