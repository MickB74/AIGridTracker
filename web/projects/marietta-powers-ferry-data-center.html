Project dossier · Marietta (Cobb County), Georgia

1155 Powers Ferry Place data center (Prime Storage site) Approved

Marietta City Council voted 5-2 on Aug 12, 2026 to approve rezoning for an 18 MW, 90,000 sq ft data center at Prime Storage's ~11-acre Powers Ferry Place property near I-75 and Delk Road — the city's second data center. Conditions include silencers on coolers/generators, an independent sound study, no connection to municipal water, and Prime Storage agreeing not to build data centers at its other Marietta sites. The application predated the six-month moratorium the council passed July 8.

Utility & grid records

The electricity request is filed with the grid operator and the utility long before the town hears about it — and states the real load.

  • No organised market covers this statenoteThere is no public queue to read. The record is the utility's integrated resource plan and its filings at the PUC — plus FERC eLibrary for anything federally docketed.
  • Georgia Public Service Commission — docket searchSpecial contracts, large-load tariffs and rate cases are docketed here. Testimony and exhibits are public, and are usually more candid than the press release.
  • FERC eLibraryFederal filings: transmission agreements, interconnection service agreements, and co-location disputes.
  • Search the serving utility for a will-serve lettersearchThe will-serve or load-service letter is the utility's own written commitment. Ask the utility and the PUC for it by name — it is frequently the first document to exist.

Federal databases

Works anywhere, and covers the operator's record at its other sites.

  • EPA ECHO facility searchEvery federally-reported air, water and waste permit at a facility, plus its inspection and violation history. Search by address or draw a radius around the site. This project's locality centres on 33.950, -84.550 — search that point with a 5-mile radius.
  • EPA EnvirofactsThe same underlying records, queryable by county — useful for 'what else is permitted near this site' rather than one facility.
  • EPA Facility Registry ServiceResolves the shell LLC on a permit to the facility it belongs to. Worth a look when the filing name means nothing locally.
