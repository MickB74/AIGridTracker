  • 2024-05-09coverage
    As of this date Meta had not yet been publicly named as the company behind Project Cosmo/Goat Systems LLC, though Fortis Construction had roughly 800 workers on site clearing the 945-acre parcel and a public announcement was planned for early July 2024. · source
  • 2024-07-02announcement
    Meta formally announced its Cheyenne data center: an $800 million, 715,000-square-foot campus at High Plains Business Park expected to support about 100 jobs, with over 1,000 construction workers at peak and a 2027 online target, built by Fortis Construction with Black Hills Energy supplying grid resources. · source
  • 2025-05-26vote
    Cheyenne City Council voted 8-1 against a proposed 12-month moratorium on new data center development, after nearly four hours of public comment citing water supply, noise, farmland conversion, and pace-of-change concerns. · source
  • 2026-01-15environmental
    The Cheyenne Board of Public Utilities discovered white bacterial colonies during routine wastewater system testing, later traced to Meta's Cheyenne Data Center contractor. · source
  • 2026-02-23environmental
    The Wyoming Public Health Laboratory identified the bacteria found in Cheyenne's reclaimed water system as Cupriavidus gilardii, an unusual soil bacterium. · source
  • 2026-03-24environmental
    Investigators confirmed the same bacteria was present at Meta's predischarge storage tank, and Cheyenne's Board of Public Utilities revoked Goat Systems LLC's industrial discharge permit for the Meta data center. · source
  • 2026-06-26environmental
    The Cheyenne Board of Public Utilities issued its first public statement on the contamination, about four months after the bacteria was first discovered, citing the Meta data center operator for 'serious noncompliance' with wastewater discharge standards and a $10,000 fine. · source
  • 2026-07-02environmental
    The Cheyenne Board of Public Utilities announced it would stop accepting industrial wastewater from data-center fill-and-flush and closed-loop cooling operations citywide following the Meta contamination incident. · source
  • 2026-07-20lawsuit
    Meta/Goat Systems filed a six-page appeal disputing the source of the bacteria contamination, asking the Cheyenne Board of Public Utilities for a contested-case hearing and a refund of the $10,000 fine. · source

Utility & grid records

The electricity request is filed with the grid operator and the utility long before the town hears about it — and states the real load.

  • Southwest Power Pool (Plains) — interconnection queueRequested megawatts, queue position and target in-service date. The MW here is the real number; the one at the town meeting is often phase one.
  • Wyoming Public Service Commission — docket searchSpecial contracts, large-load tariffs and rate cases are docketed here. Testimony and exhibits are public, and are usually more candid than the press release.
  • FERC eLibraryFederal filings: transmission agreements, interconnection service agreements, and co-location disputes.
  • Search the serving utility for a will-serve lettersearchThe will-serve or load-service letter is the utility's own written commitment. Ask the utility and the PUC for it by name — it is frequently the first document to exist.

Federal databases
