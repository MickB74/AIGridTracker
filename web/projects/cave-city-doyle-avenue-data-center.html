Cave City data center (Kentucky Industrial Alliance) Awaiting decision

Kentucky Industrial Alliance LLC proposes a data center on a 381-acre project site within its 600+ acres at 2001 Doyle Avenue, about 10 miles from Mammoth Cave National Park; it submitted an application May 11, 2026, and sued the city on June 8 after Cave City adopted a one-year moratorium. The city moved to dismiss on June 29, arguing no actual development plan was ever filed; a July 20 preliminary-injunction hearing was scheduled. Residents fear runoff through the karst into the Mammoth Cave system.
