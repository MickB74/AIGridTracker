Project dossier · Fort Stockton (Pecos County), Texas

Amazon Fort Stockton campus Under construction

Amazon Data Services filed TDLR registrations for three data center buildings (A, B, C; 189,060 sq ft and ~$300 million each) at a site 11 miles north of Fort Stockton, with construction scheduled August-December 2026, part of an August 2026 filing wave totaling 16 buildings and at least $4.8 billion across Fort Stockton, Floydada, and Amazon's existing Boling (Wharton County) campus. Amazon confirmed it is financing the GW Ranch gas plant being developed by Pacifico Energy in Pecos County (35 turbines, 7.65 GW) to power the campus; its permitted emissions of over 30 million metric tonnes of GHG per year would exceed any single source currently operating in the US.

State environmental permits

Backup generators, water withdrawal and stormwater are permitted by the state, usually earlier and in more detail than anything filed locally.

  • Texas TCEQ — Air Permits DivisionThe program page: which permits this state requires, and the office that issues them.
  • TCEQ Central RegistrySearchable public filings — search the operator, the shell LLC on the deed, and the address.

County & town filings

The rezoning application, site plan, traffic and noise studies, staff report and every revision.

  • Search Fort Stockton (Pecos County) planning filingssearchPlanning departments post application files under many different names. Find the department, then ask the clerk for the application number — that number unlocks everything else.
  • Search Fort Stockton (Pecos County) property records / GISsearchThe parcel record names the buying LLC and the sale date, which is what ties an anonymous filing to an operator.

Utility & grid records

The electricity request is filed with the grid operator and the utility long before the town hears about it — and states the real load.

  • ERCOT (Texas) — interconnection queueRequested megawatts, queue position and target in-service date. The MW here is the real number; the one at the town meeting is often phase one.
  • Public Utility Commission of Texas — docket searchSpecial contracts, large-load tariffs and rate cases are docketed here. Testimony and exhibits are public, and are usually more candid than the press release.
  • FERC eLibraryFederal filings: transmission agreements, interconnection service agreements, and co-location disputes.
  • Search the serving utility for a will-serve lettersearchThe will-serve or load-service letter is the utility's own written commitment. Ask the utility and the PUC for it by name — it is frequently the first document to exist.

Federal databases

Works anywhere, and covers the operator's record at its other sites.

  • EPA ECHO facility searchEvery federally-reported air, water and waste permit at a facility, plus its inspection and violation history. Search by address or draw a radius around the site. This project's locality centres on 30.890, -102.880 — search that point with a 5-mile radius.
  • EPA EnvirofactsThe same underlying records, queryable by county — useful for 'what else is permitted near this site' rather than one facility.
  • EPA Facility Registry ServiceResolves the shell LLC on a permit to the facility it belongs to. Worth a look when the filing name means nothing locally.

These are places to look, not evidence that anything has been filed. Links were checked on 2026-08-21; search links are queries, not records. What to ask for.
