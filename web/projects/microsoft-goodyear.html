What has happened so far (7 events)

  • 2018-08-01financing
    Microsoft purchased 279 acres in Goodyear from Carefree Partners Investments for $48 million, land later used for its data center campus south of I-10 and Phoenix Goodyear Airport. · source
  • 2018-12-01rezoning
    Goodyear City Council approved rezoning of Microsoft's parcel from final planned area development to I-1 (light industrial park) with a PAD overlay (December 2018). · source
  • 2019-03-01permit
    Goodyear City Council approved a site plan and an expedited self-certification construction process for Microsoft's project (March 2019). · source
  • 2019-05-02announcement
    Microsoft publicly revealed its Goodyear data center plans, having also acquired an additional 147 acres from DRI FR Goodyear LLC for $37 million at Citrus and Indian School roads. · source
  • 2021-06-15operational
    The first of Microsoft's three planned West Valley (Arizona) data centers, part of the West US 3 Azure region anchored by El Mirage and Goodyear campuses, went online. · source
  • 2023-03-06vote
    Goodyear City Council unanimously approved an amended development agreement requiring Microsoft's fourth and fifth buildings to use air cooling instead of direct evaporative cooling, with Microsoft committing $36 million toward a $90 million expansion of the 157th Avenue Wastewater Treatment Plant, a $5 million financial-assurance deposit for a new sewer line, and $800,000 for a city coordinator position. · source
  • 2026-06-09financing
    Microsoft paid $130 million for an additional 100-acre development site in Goodyear, about 27 miles west of Phoenix. · source

State environmental permits

Backup generators, water withdrawal and stormwater are permitted by the state, usually earlier and in more detail than anything filed locally.

  • Arizona DEQ — Air Quality DivisionThe program page: which permits this state requires, and the office that issues them.
  • Search Arizona DEQ — Air Quality Division for this projectsearchThis state publishes no searchable permit database, so a written records request to the agency is the reliable route. Use this search only to find the right office.

County & town filings

The rezoning application, site plan, traffic and noise studies, staff report and every revision.

  • Search Goodyear planning filingssearchPlanning departments post application files under many different names. Find the department, then ask the clerk for the application number — that number unlocks everything else.
  • Search Goodyear property records / GISsearchThe parcel record names the buying LLC and the sale date, which is what ties an anonymous filing to an operator.

Utility & grid records

The electricity request is filed with the grid operator and the utility long before the town hears about it — and states the real load.

  • No organised market covers this statenoteThere is no public queue to read. The record is the utility's integrated resource plan and its filings at the PUC — plus FERC eLibrary for anything federally docketed.
  • Arizona Corporation Commission — docket searchSpecial contracts, large-load tariffs and rate cases are docketed here. Testimony and exhibits are public, and are usually more candid than the press release.
  • FERC eLibraryFederal filings: transmission agreements, interconnection service agreements, and co-location disputes.
  • Search the serving utility for a will-serve lettersearchThe will-serve or load-service letter is the utility's own written commitment. Ask the utility and the PUC for it by name — it is frequently the first document to exist.

Federal databases

Works anywhere, and covers the operator's record at its other sites.

  • EPA ECHO facility searchEvery federally-reported air, water and waste permit at a facility, plus its inspection and violation history. Search by address or draw a radius around the site. This project's locality centres on 33.420, -112.360 — search that point with a 5-mile radius.
  • EPA EnvirofactsThe same underlying records, queryable by county — useful for 'what else is permitted near this site' rather than one facility.
  • EPA Facility Registry ServiceResolves the shell LLC on a permit to the facility it belongs to. Worth a look when the filing name means nothing locally.
