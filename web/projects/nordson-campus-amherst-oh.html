Nordson campus data center (Cervenka) Denied

Use variance to convert three newer buildings on the longtime Nordson Corporation campus into a data center complex - denied by the Board of Zoning Appeals on July 30, 2026, less than 24 hours after a hearing where 100+ residents packed City Hall and spilled onto the lawn. The developer's remaining path is an appeal in Lorain County Common Pleas Court.

What has happened so far (4 events)

  • 2026-04-01operational
    Nordson campus buildings at 100-300 Nordson Drive listed for sale (the property had been on the market since April, per the developer's hearing testimony), after Nordson relocated manufacturing to South Carolina. · source
  • 2026-07-14filing
    Cervenka Development Partners LLC filed a use variance application with the Amherst Board of Zoning Appeals for 100, 200, and 300 Nordson Drive, proposing to convert approximately 240,000 sq ft of former Nordson industrial space into a 45 MW data center. · source
  • 2026-07-29hearing
    Over 100 Amherst residents packed City Hall for the BZA hearing on the data center variance; fire-code capacity limits restricted entry to 60 people while others listened from the lawn on their phones; attorney Gerald Phillips argued the application was defective because Cervenka lacks ownership or documented authorization from Nordson. · source
  • 2026-07-30vote
    Amherst Board of Zoning Appeals denied the use variance, releasing its decision less than 24 hours after a packed hearing (100+ residents, capacity limits pushed the crowd outside). · source

State environmental permits

Backup generators, water withdrawal and stormwater are permitted by the state, usually earlier and in more detail than anything filed locally.

  • Ohio EPA — Division of Air Pollution ControlThe program page: which permits this state requires, and the office that issues them.
  • Ohio EPA eBusiness Center (Air Services)Searchable public filings — search the operator, the shell LLC on the deed, and the address.

County & town filings

The rezoning application, site plan, traffic and noise studies, staff report and every revision.

  • Search Amherst (Lorain County) planning filingssearchPlanning departments post application files under many different names. Find the department, then ask the clerk for the application number — that number unlocks everything else.
  • Search Amherst (Lorain County) property records / GISsearchThe parcel record names the buying LLC and the sale date, which is what ties an anonymous filing to an operator.

Utility & grid records

The electricity request is filed with the grid operator and the utility long before the town hears about it — and states the real load.

  • PJM Interconnection (Mid-Atlantic, Ohio Valley) — interconnection queueRequested megawatts, queue position and target in-service date. The MW here is the real number; the one at the town meeting is often phase one.
  • Public Utilities Commission of Ohio — docket searchSpecial contracts, large-load tariffs and rate cases are docketed here. Testimony and exhibits are public, and are usually more candid than the press release.
  • FERC eLibraryFederal filings: transmission agreements, interconnection service agreements, and co-location disputes.
  • Search the serving utility for a will-serve lettersearchThe will-serve or load-service letter is the utility's own written commitment. Ask the utility and the PUC for it by name — it is frequently the first document to exist.

Federal databases

Works anywhere, and covers the operator's record at its other sites.

  • EPA ECHO facility searchEvery federally-reported air, water and waste permit at a facility, plus its inspection and violation history. Search by address or draw a radius around the site. This project's locality centres on 41.400, -82.220 — search that point with a 5-mile radius.
  • EPA EnvirofactsThe same underlying records, queryable by county — useful for 'what else is permitted near this site' rather than one facility.
  • EPA Facility Registry ServiceResolves the shell LLC on a permit to the facility it belongs to. Worth a look when the filing name means nothing locally.

These are places to look, not evidence that anything has been filed. Links were checked on 2026-08-21; search links are queries, not records. What to ask for.
