Project dossier · Decatur Township (Indianapolis), Indiana

Sabey Decatur Township Approved

Two-building, 900,000+ sq ft, $4B campus near Camby Rd/Kentucky Ave. Approved by Metropolitan Development Commission in March 2026 via industrial-zoning adjustment; ~90 residents protested. Residents later sued; case helped drive Indianapolis's data-center moratorium push.

What has happened so far (12 events)

  • 2025-12-18announcement
    Seattle-based Sabey Data Centers sought city approval for a roughly 130-acre, two-building data center campus on industrially-zoned land near Camby Road and Kentucky Avenue in Decatur Township, telling residents the project could generate more than $20M/year in property tax revenue at full buildout. · source
  • 2025-12-18public-comment
    Residents packed a Decatur Township Civic Council meeting at Decatur Central High School to question Sabey CEO John Sabey about the proposed data center; no speaker during public comment supported the project. · source
  • 2026-01-29hearing
    The Metropolitan Development Commission's hearing examiner held a petition hearing on Sabey's request for a variance of use to allow the data center campus on industrially-zoned land in Decatur Township. · source
  • 2026-02-26hearing
    The MDC hearing examiner recommended approval of Sabey's 130-acre Decatur Township data center proposal, advancing it to the full commission despite community opposition. · source
  • 2026-02-26protest
    About 90 Decatur Township residents demonstrated outside the City-County Building against the Sabey data center, citing noise, energy demand, water use, and property-value concerns; 1,400+ had signed an opposition petition vs. 24 supporters. · source
  • 2026-03-18vote
    The Indianapolis Metropolitan Development Commission voted to approve Sabey's variance-of-use request for a $4B, two-building, 900,000+ sq ft, ~250MW data center campus on 130 acres, making it Indianapolis's first hyperscale data center. · source
  • 2026-04-17lawsuit
    The Decatur Township Civic Council and more than half a dozen residents filed a petition for judicial review in Marion County Superior Court to overturn the MDC's approval, arguing the variance violated state law and due-process rights and amounted to a 'disguised rezoning.' · source
  • 2026-04-20coverage
    Coverage confirmed Decatur Township residents sued to block the $4B Sabey data center, citing noise, energy demand, water use, lack of permanent jobs, and property-value impacts. · source
  • 2026-06-15filing
    Attorneys for Sabey and the MDC filed a motion to dismiss the residents' judicial-review petition, arguing the citizens' petition failed to meet statutory requirements. · source
  • 2026-07-10coverage
    Council President Maggie A. Lewis announced she would seek a citywide data-center moratorium, citing the Sabey fight among the drivers of the push. · source
  • 2026-08-10moratorium
    Indianapolis City-County Council voted 23-1 to advance a moratorium pausing approval of new data centers in Marion County through 2028, partly driven by the Sabey fight; the measure exempts the three already-approved projects including Sabey's. · source
  • 2026-08-19moratorium
    The Metropolitan Development Commission voted 6-0 to give final approval to the citywide data-center moratorium (through Dec. 31, 2027); Sabey's Decatur Township campus was one of three already-approved projects explicitly exempted under state law. · source

State environmental permits

Backup generators, water withdrawal and stormwater are permitted by the state, usually earlier and in more detail than anything filed locally.

  • Indiana IDEM — Office of Air QualityThe program page: which permits this state requires, and the office that issues them.
  • IDEM Virtual File CabinetSearchable public filings — search the operator, the shell LLC on the deed, and the address.

County & town filings

The rezoning application, site plan, traffic and noise studies, staff report and every revision.

  • Search Decatur Township (Indianapolis) planning filingssearchPlanning departments post application files under many different names. Find the department, then ask the clerk for the application number — that number unlocks everything else.
  • Search Decatur Township (Indianapolis) property records / GISsearchThe parcel record names the buying LLC and the sale date, which is what ties an anonymous filing to an operator.

Utility & grid records

The electricity request is filed with the grid operator and the utility long before the town hears about it — and states the real load.

  • MISO (Midwest & Gulf) — interconnection queueRequested megawatts, queue position and target in-service date. The MW here is the real number; the one at the town meeting is often phase one.
  • PJM Interconnection (Mid-Atlantic, Ohio Valley) — interconnection queueRequested megawatts, queue position and target in-service date. The MW here is the real number; the one at the town meeting is often phase one.
  • Indiana Utility Regulatory Commission — docket searchSpecial contracts, large-load tariffs and rate cases are docketed here. Testimony and exhibits are public, and are usually more candid than the press release.
  • FERC eLibraryFederal filings: transmission agreements, interconnection service agreements, and co-location disputes.
  • Search the serving utility for a will-serve lettersearchThe will-serve or load-service letter is the utility's own written commitment. Ask the utility and the PUC for it by name — it is frequently the first document to exist.

Federal databases

Works anywhere, and covers the operator's record at its other sites.

  • EPA ECHO facility searchEvery federally-reported air, water and waste permit at a facility, plus its inspection and violation history. Search by address or draw a radius around the site.
  • EPA EnvirofactsThe same underlying records, queryable by county — useful for 'what else is permitted near this site' rather than one facility.
  • EPA Facility Registry ServiceResolves the shell LLC on a permit to the facility it belongs to. Worth a look when the filing name means nothing locally.

These are places to look, not evidence that anything has been filed. Links were checked on 2026-08-21; search links are queries, not records. What to ask for.
