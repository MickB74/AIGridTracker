What has happened so far (11 events)

  • 2007-06-01announcement
    In June 2007, Google announced a $600 million investment to build a data center at a former drive-in theater site on Veterans Memorial Highway in Council Bluffs, with the first phase targeted for completion in 2009. · source
  • 2013-10-03operational
    Google held a grand opening for its second Council Bluffs campus on Bunge Avenue, starting with 50 employees focused on installing, upgrading, and maintaining servers. · source
  • 2014-03-28announcement
    Google announced a third expansion phase (Southlands) in Council Bluffs, creating 35 additional positions and bringing total investment to $1.5 billion, the largest private investment in Iowa history at that time; the state increased Google's sales and use tax abatement from $9.6 million to $16.8 million. · source
  • 2023-08-08announcement
    Google announced a $350 million expansion of its Council Bluffs data center, the third expansion since 2007, along with a $250,000 grant to Advance Southwest Iowa; the campus then spanned over 2.5 million square feet east of Interstate 29 on Bunge Avenue. · source
  • 2024-07-02announcement
    Google announced an additional $1 billion investment in its Council Bluffs data center plus $1.3 million for water preservation in the Missouri River Valley Basin, bringing the campus to approximately 3 million square feet and total investment to over $6 billion. · source
  • 2025-05-30announcement
    Google announced a $7 billion investment in Iowa over the next two years for a new data center in Cedar Rapids and expansion of its existing Council Bluffs facility, plus a partnership with the Electrical Training Alliance to increase Iowa's electrical workforce pipeline by 95 percent. · source
  • 2025-10-27utility
    Google and NextEra Energy announced a collaboration to restart the 615 MW Duane Arnold nuclear plant in Iowa under a 25-year power purchase agreement, with operations expected by Q1 2029, to support Google's expanding Iowa data center operations. · source
  • 2026-06-03environmental
    Google gave $3 million to Omaha's Metropolitan Utilities District to install acoustic leak detection sensors across 500 miles of high-priority water lines, part of a $17 million water stewardship initiative across seven states including Iowa where Google operates data centers. · source
  • 2026-06-18vote
    The Council Bluffs City Council rejected Mayor Jill Shudak's proposed 12-month moratorium on new data center construction; Public Works Director Matt Cox noted data centers request one to two million gallons of water per day and individual projects could max out sewer capacity. · source
  • 2026-07-08report
    Flatwater Free Press reported that Google self-reported its Council Bluffs facility consumed 1.3 billion gallons of water in 2025, more than any of its other data center locations worldwide that year. · source
  • 2026-08-04construction
    A construction apprentice was trapped under an elevator at Google Southlands in Council Bluffs and airlifted to a hospital after the Council Bluffs Fire Department extracted him; the incident occurred at approximately 5 p.m. during ongoing construction work. · source

Find the paper trail (0 filed · 12 places to look)

State environmental permits

Backup generators, water withdrawal and stormwater are permitted by the state, usually earlier and in more detail than anything filed locally.

  • Iowa DNR — Air Quality BureauThe program page: which permits this state requires, and the office that issues them.
  • Iowa DNR document searchSearchable public filings — search the operator, the shell LLC on the deed, and the address.

County & town filings

The rezoning application, site plan, traffic and noise studies, staff report and every revision.

  • Search Council Bluffs planning filingssearchPlanning departments post application files under many different names. Find the department, then ask the clerk for the application number — that number unlocks everything else.
  • Search Council Bluffs property records / GISsearchThe parcel record names the buying LLC and the sale date, which is what ties an anonymous filing to an operator.

Utility & grid records

The electricity request is filed with the grid operator and the utility long before the town hears about it — and states the real load.

  • MISO (Midwest & Gulf) — interconnection queueRequested megawatts, queue position and target in-service date. The MW here is the real number; the one at the town meeting is often phase one.
  • Southwest Power Pool (Plains) — interconnection queueRequested megawatts, queue position and target in-service date. The MW here is the real number; the one at the town meeting is often phase one.
  • Iowa Utilities Commission — docket searchSpecial contracts, large-load tariffs and rate cases are docketed here. Testimony and exhibits are public, and are usually more candid than the press release.
  • FERC eLibraryFederal filings: transmission agreements, interconnection service agreements, and co-location disputes.
  • Search the serving utility for a will-serve lettersearchThe will-serve or load-service letter is the utility's own written commitment. Ask the utility and the PUC for it by name — it is frequently the first document to exist.

Federal databases

Works anywhere, and covers the operator's record at its other sites.

  • EPA ECHO facility searchEvery federally-reported air, water and waste permit at a facility, plus its inspection and violation history. Search by address or draw a radius around the site. This project's locality centres on 41.220, -95.860 — search that point with a 5-mile radius.
  • EPA EnvirofactsThe same underlying records, queryable by county — useful for 'what else is permitted near this site' rather than one facility.
  • EPA Facility Registry ServiceResolves the shell LLC on a permit to the facility it belongs to. Worth a look when the filing name means nothing locally.

These are places to look, not evidence that anything has been filed. Links were checked on 2026-08-21; search links are queries, not records. What to ask for.
