Talen Energy Montour Data Center Denied

County commissioners unanimously denied Talen Energy's request to rezone 800+ acres from agricultural to industrial for a data center plus power-plant expansion, after a grassroots group gathered ~3,000 petition signatures.

What has happened so far (13 events)

  • 2024-11-01ruling
    FERC rejected a proposed interconnection service agreement among PJM, PPL Electric, and Talen that would have increased the load Talen could transfer directly from the Susquehanna nuclear plant to its co-located Amazon data center campus. · source
  • 2025-06-01utility
    Talen Energy expanded its Amazon Web Services partnership with a new long-term agreement to supply up to 1,920 MWe from its Susquehanna nuclear plant through 2042, part of Talen's broader co-location strategy for AI data centers in Pennsylvania. · source
  • 2025-08-01announcement
    Concerned Citizens of Montour County formed with four members after Talen's rezoning request became public; its first town hall days later drew about 120 people. · source
  • 2025-08-18filing
    Talen Energy submitted a rezoning petition to Montour County covering roughly 870 acres in Anthony and Derry Townships, seeking to change land from agricultural to industrial near its Montour Steam Electric Station for a data center and power-plant expansion. · source
  • 2025-11-19hearing
    The Montour County Planning Commission voted to recommend against Talen Energy's request to rezone roughly 870 acres of agricultural land to industrial. · source
  • 2026-01-23hearing
    Montour County commissioners held an all-day public hearing at First Baptist Church of Danville on Talen's rezoning request; residents wearing red 'Say No Industrial Rezoning' shirts testified about farmland loss, water use, noise, and land impact. · source
  • 2026-01-23public-comment
    Concerned Citizens of Montour County delivered a petition signed by about 3,000 residents opposing the rezoning, roughly twice the population of the township and more than voted there in the 2024 election. · source
  • 2026-02-10denial
    Montour County's three commissioners voted unanimously to deny Talen Energy's rezoning request; Chair Rebecca Dressler said Talen had not met its burden of proving the project would benefit the community and that no evidence showed benefits outweighed foreseeable public harms. · source
  • 2026-02-10coverage
    Talen Energy responded saying it remains committed to pursuing a data center in Montour County and would 'listen, incorporate feedback from the Commissioners, engage with the community, and refine our plans.' · source
  • 2026-03-30report
    Civil Eats profiled the Montour County fight as a case study of a small farm county organizing successfully against a hyperscale data center and power-plant expansion. · source
  • 2026-04-14public-comment
    Montour County held a public hearing on proposed amendments to its data center ordinance, including new height and setback distance requirements. · source
  • 2026-04-28ruling
    Montour County commissioners unanimously approved amended data center ordinance provisions establishing new height and setback requirements for any future data center development. · source
  • 2026-08-18moratorium
    Governor Josh Shapiro signed an executive order restricting data center development in Pennsylvania, eliminating fast-tracked permitting and formalizing GRID Standards requiring community engagement, energy-affordability protections, and environmental standards for developers seeking state support. · source

Find the paper trail (0 filed · 12 places to look)

State environmental permits

Backup generators, water withdrawal and stormwater are permitted by the state, usually earlier and in more detail than anything filed locally.

  • Pennsylvania DEPThe program page: which permits this state requires, and the office that issues them.
  • DEP Data Center Permit Tracker (the only state register built for data centers — it already feeds this tracker)Searchable public filings — search the operator, the shell LLC on the deed, and the address.

County & town filings

The rezoning application, site plan, traffic and noise studies, staff report and every revision.

  • Board of Commissioners agendas & packetsThe meeting packet carries the staff report, site plan and conditions — verified page for this locality.
  • Search Montour County planning filingssearchPlanning departments post application files under many different names. Find the department, then ask the clerk for the application number — that number unlocks everything else.
  • Search Montour County property records / GISsearchThe parcel record names the buying LLC and the sale date, which is what ties an anonymous filing to an operator.

Utility & grid records

The electricity request is filed with the grid operator and the utility long before the town hears about it — and states the real load.

  • PJM Interconnection (Mid-Atlantic, Ohio Valley) — interconnection queueRequested megawatts, queue position and target in-service date. The MW here is the real number; the one at the town meeting is often phase one.
  • Pennsylvania Public Utility Commission — docket searchSpecial contracts, large-load tariffs and rate cases are docketed here. Testimony and exhibits are public, and are usually more candid than the press release.
  • FERC eLibraryFederal filings: transmission agreements, interconnection service agreements, and co-location disputes.
  • Search the serving utility for a will-serve lettersearchThe will-serve or load-service letter is the utility's own written commitment. Ask the utility and the PUC for it by name — it is frequently the first document to exist.

Federal databases

Works anywhere, and covers the operator's record at its other sites.

  • EPA ECHO facility searchEvery federally-reported air, water and waste permit at a facility, plus its inspection and violation history. Search by address or draw a radius around the site.
  • EPA EnvirofactsThe same underlying records, queryable by county — useful for 'what else is permitted near this site' rather than one facility.
  • EPA Facility Registry ServiceResolves the shell LLC on a permit to the facility it belongs to. Worth a look when the filing name means nothing locally.

These are places to look, not evidence that anything has been filed. Links were checked on 2026-08-21; search links are queries, not records. What to ask for.
