Communities that organized, won

  • The Dalles, OR (Google) — $29M wastewater treatment upgrade + recycled-water cooling; city capped DC draws at 25% of municipal supply
  • Groton, CT (Vantage) — $2.5M community recreation center CBA, recorded as a zoning condition after a year-long moratorium

Every one of these happened before final approval. Timing is the leverage.
