QTS (Blackstone)

>2 GW contracted capacity · disclosure: partial

Context

Private since 2021 (Blackstone, $10B). Reports strong water and clean-energy metrics but omits fleet PUE and audited emissions totals.

What they do report

WUE0.82 L/kWh (−27% YoY)
Carbon-free electricity100%
Water-free new builds100% of greenfield

What they don't report

  • Fleet PUE
  • Scope 1/2/3 (absolute)
  • Total electricity (TWh)
  • Revenue

Why this matters

Companies building gigawatts of data center capacity without publishing environmental data are asking communities to approve projects on trust. If a developer won't disclose PUE, water consumption, and emissions before construction, that's a question for your planning board.
