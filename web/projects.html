Plymouth Township (Montgomery County), PA · 66 ac · Owner: Brian O'Neill (developer) · Filing LLC: 900 Conshohocken Road LLC

900 Conshohocken Road LLC, an entity of developer Brian O'Neill, seeks a special exception from Plymouth Township's zoning hearing board to convert the recently closed Cleveland-Cliffs steel mill — 10 existing buildings totaling about 2 million sq ft on 66 acres along the Schuylkill River — into a data center. The board set a procedural-only session for Aug. 17, with testimony continuing in September; an opposition petition has topped 4,000 signatures.

Manse Technology Campus (formerly Project Blackjack) Hearing scheduled

Pahrump (Nye County), NV · 900 ac · Operator: Gigawatt Development

Gigawatt Development proposes a ~900-acre, multi-phase technology campus south of Carpenter Canyon Road along SR-160 at the south end of Pahrump Valley, pledging closed-loop cooling using under 300 acre-feet of water per year (~1.5% of the Pahrump Basin's annual yield) on water rights it already owns, with no tax abatements sought; the application predates the county's June 2026 temporary moratorium on new data center applications.
