How this list is built — and how to add to it

A weekly scan of local news and known megaprojects surfaces leads into a review queue; a human reads the governing body's own agenda or the reporting before anything lands here, which is where each row's source and date come from. That means this is a working set, not a census — most of the country's activity isn't in here yet.
