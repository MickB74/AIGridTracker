The projects on the table

Compass Datacenters Red Oak campus — Approved

Operator: Compass Datacenters · Acres: 830

Red Oak City Council voted 4-1 to rezone ~830 acres of agricultural land ~20 miles south of Dallas to a planned development for a Compass data center campus of nearly a dozen buildings plus substations, after a marathon May 11 meeting that ran past midnight with 150+ residents present and ~1,600 petition signatures against; the planning and zoning board had recommended denial in late April. Conditions include lighting shields, decibel limits, a cryptocurrency-mining ban, closed-loop cooling with no city water draw, and $72 million in electric grid reinforcements; a 10-year, $2.82 million tax abatement passed on a separate 4-1 vote.

Next: Approved — pivot from stopping it to a binding CBA: enforceable caps on noise, water and cost, plus a community benefit.

Operator: DataBank · Size: 300 MW

8-building campus (400MW substation, up to 300MW critical IT); first 3 buildings already leased, $2.0B construction financing secured April 2026.

Next: Under construction — pull the approved conditions and permits and hold the builder to them; an expansion or a new building reopens the record.
