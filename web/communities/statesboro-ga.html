The project on the table

Burkhalter Road data center (4AM Development) — Awaiting decision

Filing LLC: 4AM Development, LLC · Acres: 26

Two-story 230,000 sq ft data center plus a 32,000 sq ft substation on ~26.5 acres at 6539 Burkhalter Road (R-4 residential to Office & Business rezoning). Planning Commission recommended approval 5-1; City Council decides Aug 18, 2026. Bulloch County, separately, has been extending data-center moratoriums.

Next: Heard 2026-08-18; decision pending. Call the board clerk for the vote date and get on the notification list.
