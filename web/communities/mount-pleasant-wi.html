Latest in the record

  • 2026-07-02 — Three Sturtevant residents filed a class-action lawsuit against Microsoft in U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Wisconsin, alleging significant noise pollution from diesel generators and HVAC systems at the Fairwater data center, seeking to represent over 1,000 households within 1.5 miles. · source
  • 2026-06-23 — Microsoft announced the Fairwater data center in Mount Pleasant is fully operational, calling it 'the world's most powerful supercomputer'; the 1.2-million-square-foot facility employs approximately 550 full-time workers and was built by nearly 10,000 construction workers over two years. · source
  • 2026-04-01 — In April 2026, Microsoft brought equipment online and began startup activities at the Fairwater data center, its first facility in Mount Pleasant. · source
  • 2026-01-26 — Mount Pleasant Village Board unanimously approved site plans for 15 additional Microsoft data centers on two campuses (9 buildings on 791 acres near Durand Avenue, 6 buildings on 530 acres along International Drive), with anticipated power demand of approximately 2 gigawatts when fully developed. · source
  • 2026-01-20 — Microsoft proposed a $13.3 billion expansion in Mount Pleasant with two new campuses totaling 15 data centers across more than 1,300 acres, potentially generating over $76 million in annual property tax revenue. · source
  • 2025-09-29 — Caledonia Plan Commission voted 5-2 to rezone 245 acres from agricultural to light industrial for Microsoft's third data center (Project Nova) near the We Energies Oak Creek Power Plant, after more than 40 residents spoke against it. · source
