The project on the table

900 Conshohocken Road data center (former Cleveland-Cliffs steel mill) — Awaiting decision

Owner: Brian O'Neill (developer) · Filing LLC: 900 Conshohocken Road LLC · Acres: 66

900 Conshohocken Road LLC, an entity of developer Brian O'Neill, seeks a special exception from Plymouth Township's zoning hearing board to convert the recently closed Cleveland-Cliffs steel mill — 10 existing buildings totaling about 2 million sq ft on 66 acres along the Schuylkill River — into a data center. The Aug. 6 hearing nearly filled a 750-seat auditorium; Conshohocken Borough was granted party status while Norristown was denied. The board set a procedural-only session for Aug. 17, with testimony continuing in September; an opposition petition has topped 4,000 signatures.

Next: Heard 2026-08-17; decision pending. Call the board clerk for the vote date and get on the notification list.

Where the decision gets made

Zoning Hearing Board — Special-exception and variance applications, including developer Brian O'Neill's proposal to convert the former Cleveland-Cliffs steel mill at 900 Conshohocken Road into a 2-million-sq-ft data center. Township Council has separately issued a public statement opposing the project and listed 43 conditions it says the proposal would need to meet.

Meets: Hearings are scheduled individually and announced on the township website; recent sessions have been held at Colonial Middle School, 716 Belvoir Road, Plymouth Meeting, PA.
Where: Colonial Middle School, 716 Belvoir Road, Plymouth Meeting, PA 19462

Public comment: Public comment is not taken at procedural/opening hearings; the township announces in advance which session will take resident testimony. Use the Citizen Request option at plymouthtownship.org/citizen-action-line/ for general contact.
