Amazon Madison Mega Site — Operating

Operator: Amazon · Owner: Amazon · Tenant: Anthropic · Size: 284 MW

Part of Project Rainier. Chip types: Trainium2. Epoch AI estimates ~284 MW.

Next: Already operating — the leverage now is enforcement: noise and water conditions, tax revenue tracking, and any expansion filing, which reopens the record.

Latest in the record

  • 2026-06-09 — Of Amazon four announced Mississippi data center facilities, the Canton campus in Madison County was reported as the one currently operating, with more than 700 diesel generators planned across the two Madison County campuses and the Canton facility set to transition to 100% recycled wastewater for cooling by 2027. · source
  • 2026-04-22 — Canton residents described construction dust, noise and pollution fears; Madison County constable Johnny Sims said construction dust had worsened his teenage daughter's asthma, and Amazon stated it is committed to being a responsible community partner. · source
  • 2026-04-10 — Coverage noted Amazon's environmental commitments: Canton will use recycled water for cooling, the Ridgeland campus will draw groundwater only 9% of the time, and the Clinton facility will operate without water, alongside plans for over 800 diesel generators across the Madison County campuses. · source
  • 2026-04-09 — Governor Tate Reeves and Amazon announced an additional $11 billion expansion of Madison County data center operations (700 new jobs) plus a $1 billion facility in Clinton/Hinds County (100 jobs), bringing Amazon total Mississippi investment commitment to $25 billion and over 2,000 jobs. · source
  • 2026-02-19 — Residents of the Idlebriar subdivision in Canton, next to the Amazon data center construction site, reported two years of construction dust, noise and traffic, with one resident's teenage daughter having multiple ER visits for asthma flare-ups the prior year, while the Madison County Economic Development Authority said it lacks funds to buy out the subdivision. · source
  • 2025-10-29 — AWS's Project Rainier — the AI training cluster powering Anthropic's Claude, spread across Pennsylvania, Indiana, and Mississippi — was reported operational, with its Mississippi campus near Canton in Madison County cited at more than 350 MW of IT capacity and set to ramp past 1 GW. · source
