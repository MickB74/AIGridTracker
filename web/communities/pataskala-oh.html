The project on the table

Aligned Data Centers - Pataskala industrial park — Awaiting decision

Operator: Aligned Data Centers · Size: 200 MW · Acres: 89

200 MW campus in three buildings on ~89 acres at Broad St SW & Mink St SW. Planning & Zoning recommended rejection 5-2 after protests filled City Hall; City Council decides Aug 25, 2026 (5:30 p.m.). Council has separately advanced a ballot measure that could ban large data centers.

Next: Heard 2026-08-25; decision pending. Call the board clerk for the vote date and get on the notification list.

Latest in the record

  • 2026-08-25 — Pataskala City Council public hearing on the site plan, 5:30 p.m., council chambers - the decisive vote. · source
  • 2026-08-03 — Pataskala City Council passed an ordinance placing a charter amendment that could ban large data centers on the November ballot, expediting the vote to meet the Aug. 5 filing deadline for the Nov. 3 ballot. · source
  • 2026-06-30 — The Columbus Dispatch reported that Pataskala planning and zoning staff had recommended approval of Aligned's 200-megawatt plan, but the planning and zoning commission voted 5-2 to recommend rejection, sending the decision to City Council's Aug. 25 hearing. · source

  • 2026-06-04 — Ohio Free Press reported that residents at the June 3 zoning meeting raised concerns the campus would use Bloom Energy fuel cells requiring roughly 15,000 decatherms of natural gas and cited noise from a comparable Aligned facility in Sandusky, Ohio. · source
