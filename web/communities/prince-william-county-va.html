PW Digital Gateway — Withdrawn

Operator: Compass Datacenters / QTS · Acres: 2,100

Approved in 2023 (4-3-1) but the rezoning was judicially voided over defective hearing notice; county and developer dropped their appeals in 2026, ending the project as approved.

Next: Withdrawn — confirm it is dead in the record and watch for the same site resurfacing under a new name or LLC.

Google Bristow — Operating

Operator: Google · Owner: Google · Tenant: Google DeepMind · Size: 279 MW

Chip types: TPU v5e,TPU v5p,TPU v6e,TPU v7. Epoch AI estimates ~279 MW.

Next: Already operating — the leverage now is enforcement: noise and water conditions, tax revenue tracking, and any expansion filing, which reopens the record.

Latest in the record

  • 2025-08-28 — The Prince William Times reported that Google's Mango Farm data center complex at 13001 Rollins Ford Road in Bristow, next to Gainesville High School, occupies 250 acres with one operational data center of about 290,000 square feet, is valued at $1.2 billion by Prince William County, and has plans filed for at least two more data centers on the site. · source
  • 2025-08-27 — Google announced an additional $9 billion investment in Virginia through the end of 2026 for cloud and AI infrastructure, including expansion of existing facilities in Prince William County; the Bristow Mango Farm complex is included in the investment, per the Prince William Times. · source
  • 2024-04-26 — Google announced a $1 billion investment to expand its Virginia data centers, including its newly opened Prince William County campus and two Loudoun County sites, bringing total Virginia investment to over $4.2 billion; Governor Youngkin attended the announcement at Google's Reston headquarters. · source
  • 2024-03-07 — The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers approved Sharpless Enterprises' application for the 181-acre data center complex at 6201 Wellington Road near Bristow, after reviewing potential impacts on nearby wetlands and streams; Corps biologist Anna Lawston said nothing substantial changed between the initial application and final approval. · source
  • 2023-11-01 — In November 2023, Delaware-based Sharpless Enterprises LLC, a shell company linked to Google, submitted plans to the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers for a 181-acre data center campus with four buildings, an electric substation, and auxiliary structures on Wellington Road in western Prince William County, on the former Atlantic Research Corporation site. · source
  • 2023-01-01 — Google's Prince William County data center campus in Bristow, a roughly 290,000-square-foot facility on the 250-acre Mango Farm site at 13001 Rollins Ford Road, was built and became operational in 2023, according to Virginia Business. · source
