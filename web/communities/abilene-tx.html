Stargate Abilene — Approved

Operator: Crusoe · Owner: OpenAI / Oracle / SoftBank (Stargate) · Tenant: OpenAI / Oracle · Size: 1,200 MW

The flagship Stargate site, built by Crusoe on Lancium's Clean Campus - the most complete Stargate location, with roughly four of eight buildings operational (~0.3 GW live) as of mid-2026. OpenAI and Oracle capped the site at 1.2 GW in March 2026, dropping further expansion amid grid-interconnection delays exceeding a year. Power is a mix of on-site natural gas and grid supply.

Next: Approved — pivot from stopping it to a binding CBA: enforceable caps on noise, water and cost, plus a community benefit.
