The projects on the table

AWS Salem Township campus (Susquehanna nuclear plant) — Under construction

Operator: Amazon (AWS) · Owner: Amazon (AWS) · Tenant: Amazon (AWS) · Size: 960 MW

One of two initial sites in Amazon's announced $20B+ Pennsylvania data-center investment: a campus alongside the Susquehanna Steam Electric Station nuclear plant, where majority owner Talen Energy agreed to supply 960 MW. The original behind-the-meter connection drew FERC scrutiny and was blocked in 2024. The 960 MW figure is the contracted power supply, not built capacity.

Next: Under construction — pull the approved conditions and permits and hold the builder to them; an expansion or a new building reopens the record.

Latest in the record

  • 2026-03-25 — QTS (owned by Blackstone) completed acquisition of approximately 1,700 acres from 96 Salem Township landowners for over $500 million, planning a 12-to-17 building data center campus adjacent to the AWS site using water-free cooling technology. · source
  • 2025-10-01 — FERC argued in an October 2025 brief to the Fifth Circuit that Talen's lawsuit should be dismissed as moot because the company had already contracted to abandon the behind-the-meter co-location arrangement at issue in the appeal. · source
  • 2025-06-11 — Talen Energy and Amazon finalized a restructured 17-year, $18 billion power purchase agreement supplying up to 1,920 MW from Susquehanna via a grid-connected front-of-meter retail structure, bypassing the rejected behind-the-meter model; deliveries to ramp to 840-1,200 MW by 2029 and 1,680-1,920 MW by 2032. · source
  • 2025-06-09 — Amazon and Gov. Shapiro announced at least $20 billion for data center campuses in Pennsylvania, with initial sites in Salem Township (alongside the Susquehanna nuclear plant, with Talen Energy agreeing to provide 960 MW) and Falls Township, creating at least 1,250 permanent jobs. · source
  • 2025-06-01 — Spotlight PA reported in June 2025 that Amazon's data centers qualify for Pennsylvania's sales tax exemption on data center equipment (enacted 2016, expanded 2021), with the state projecting $43 million in lost revenue for fiscal year 2025-26; critics noted no mandatory reporting of exempt transaction costs. · source
  • 2025-04-10 — FERC upheld its rejection of the amended interconnection service agreement between PJM and Susquehanna Nuclear on rehearing, reaffirming its November 2024 order blocking expanded behind-the-meter power sales to the AWS data center. · source
