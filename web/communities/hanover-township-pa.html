The project on the table

Prime Data Centers — Starpointe Business Park campus — Awaiting decision

Operator: Prime Data Centers · Filing LLC: Prime Data Centers LLC · Size: 450 MW · Acres: 126

Prime Data Centers filed a conditional-use application in May 2026 for a seven-building, ~1.7 million sq ft data center campus with an attached behind-the-meter 450 MW gas-fired power plant on roughly 126 acres of reclaimed strip-mine land in the Starpointe Business Park (land owned by ALTIAN Development LLC), near the Pavilion at Starlake. The Aug. 5 conditional-use hearing drew 200+ residents; about three dozen won party status, and residents' attorneys argued the application belongs before the zoning hearing board rather than the supervisors, so the hearing was continued to Aug. 31 without Prime presenting.

Next: Heard 2026-08-31; decision pending. Call the board clerk for the vote date and get on the notification list.
