The data center fight in Grant County, Indiana

The projects on the table

Project Riverjump (Marion) — Withdrawn

Code-named hyperscale data center proposed for the west side of the City of Marion, the county seat. Because Marion is incorporated, the county's 24-month moratorium (Ordinance 4-2026) never applied to it. Rumors of a data center west of Marion near Central Indiana Ethanol circulated from January 2026; Mayor Ronald Morrell Jr. said then that no ordinance or vote was scheduled. In late August 2026, citing public feedback in person and online, Morrell said he would not pursue or support data center development in Marion under his administration. The developer and end user were never publicly identified; no application appears in the public record.

Next: Withdrawn — confirm it is dead in the record and watch for the same site resurfacing under a new name or LLC.

Recore Digital AI data center inquiry — Rumored

Operator: Recore Digital

An inquiry, not an application. Recore Digital approached the Grant County Area Plan Commission in July 2026 about converting an existing cryptocurrency mining operation in the county into what it describes as a 'quiet, permanent AI Data Center'. The county's 24-month moratorium (Ordinance 4-2026) bars improvement-location permits for data centers in unincorporated areas until March 2028 or until the APC's data center ordinance is adopted, so the live question is what that ordinance will require; a seven-member subcommittee (four APC members, three residents) finished its first review of the draft in August 2026. Site, size, power and water figures have not been published. Coordinates are the county centroid.

Next: Rumored only. Confirm with the planning office and file a records request to pin down what is real.

Where the decision gets made

Grant County Board of Commissioners — County ordinances for unincorporated Grant County, including the 24-month data center moratorium (Ordinance 4-2026) and the zoning amendments the Area Plan Commission recommends to end it. The Area Plan Commission (first Monday, 7 p.m., same chambers; (765) 668-4765, areaplan@grantcounty.in.gov) drafts the data center ordinance and holds the advertised public hearing on it. The seven-member County Council (countycouncil@grantcounty.in.gov, (765) 668-8871) controls the budget and any tax abatement.

Meets: 1st and 3rd Monday, 10:00 a.m. (following Tuesday when Monday is a holiday)
Where: Council Chambers, Grant County Complex, 401 S. Adams Street, Marion, IN 46953

Public comment: Every regular session ends with a public-comments item and the minutes record speakers by name and town. Meetings stream on Webex (see the county page for the access code) and past sessions are on the Grant County Government YouTube channel. Special meetings are posted on the county building doors 48 hours ahead.
