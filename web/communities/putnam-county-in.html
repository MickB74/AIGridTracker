The data center fight in Putnam County, Indiana

As of September 12, 2026, Putnam County, Indiana has a data center moratorium in force (action taken Nov 17, 2025; documented end date 2026-11-17).

The project on the table

Cloverdale Solar Farm (EnergyRe) — Withdrawn

Operator: EnergyRe · Size: 150 MW · Acres: 1,000

Revised proposal for a 150 MW solar farm (cut from an original ~200 MW / ~2,000 acres) in the Cloverdale area, with increased setbacks (500 ft). A Plan Commission hearing was scheduled for Jan 12, 2026 but the county's moratorium (adopted Nov 17, 2025) paused new approvals; EnergyRe later withdrew the application per the tracker's existing record. This is a solar project, not a data center — no separate data-center proposal in Putnam County was found in this research pass.

Where the decision gets made

Putnam County Board of Commissioners — Zoning/UDO ordinances (including the SMR/data-center/solar/wind moratorium), rezoning and development applications in unincorporated Putnam County, county administrative and budget matters

Meets: Regular sessions historically held on a recurring schedule at the Putnam County Courthouse, Room 10 (1st floor); as of July 1, 2025 meetings are livestreamed. Exact recurring day/time was not confirmed on the county site as of this research pass — verify via the Commissioner Meeting Dates page below before publishing a specific day.
Where: Putnam County Courthouse, 1 Courthouse Square, Greencastle, IN 46135 (Commissioners' Room)

Public comment: Public comment is taken during regular Commissioners' meetings; the Nov 17, 2025 moratorium vote followed public hearing testimony, including from residents and an attorney representing opponents of the EnergyRe project.
