The project on the table

BCG Cedar Creek Campus — Awaiting decision

Operator: BlackChamber Group · Filing LLC: Black Chamber Partners · Acres: 2,842

Black Chamber Partners (a BlackChamber Group affiliate) and co-developer Pacifico Energy plan a 2,842-acre data center campus at 382 Earl Callahan Road near Cedar Creek with a 710 MW gas-fired energy facility, a ~$2.2 billion investment with two 100% county tax-abatement applications under review; construction is estimated for September 2026 through October 2028. Neighboring property owners filed motions July 20 asking TCEQ to overturn two construction stormwater permits issued in mid-July.

Next: Heard 2026-08-24; decision pending. Call the board clerk for the vote date and get on the notification list.
