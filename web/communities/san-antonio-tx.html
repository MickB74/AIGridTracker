The data center fight in San Antonio, Texas

As of September 6, 2026, San Antonio, Texas rejected a data center moratorium (action taken Aug 26, 2026).

Status is derived, not stored — a pause with a documented end date flips to Expired on its own — and the source is right next to the claim.

Aug 26, 2026action taken
Locallevel

Three council members (Galvan, Castillo, Meza Gonzalez) filed an Aug 7 memo for a 3-6 month moratorium. At the Aug 26 session most of council declined it by consensus and chose UDC amendments instead: 500-ft buffers from homes, schools and hospitals, industrial/commercial zones only, noise buffers, possible special-use authorization.
