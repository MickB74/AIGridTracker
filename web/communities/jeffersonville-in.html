The data center fight in Jeffersonville, Indiana

As of September 8, 2026, Jeffersonville, Indiana has a data center moratorium in force (action taken Aug 4, 2026; documented end date 2027-08-04).

Status is derived, not stored — a pause with a documented end date flips to Expired on its own — and the source is right next to the claim.

Status today

Aug 4, 2026action taken
Locallevel

City Council voted 8-0 on Aug 4, 2026; the administration's original ordinance set a 6-month pause, and at-large member Jacky Snelling moved to extend it to a full year before passage. City Attorney Les Merkley told the council Indiana law does not allow an outright ban, only regulation, so the Plan Commission is drafting data-center standards for the city's Unified Development Ordinance during the pause, targeting adoption before Jan 1, 2027. District 1's Dustin White proposed a River Ridge-coterminous data-center overlay district as one option. Does not affect the Meta facility at River Ridge Commerce Center, where the River Ridge Development Authority — not the city — holds land-use authority under a decades-old interlocal agreement rooted in the site's history as the former Indiana Army Ammunition Plant

Latest in the record

  • 2026-08-26 — Louisville Public Media explained how the Meta data center was approved outside normal city zoning: Jeffersonville's 2022 development agreement with 'Blocke LLC' (a Meta shell entity) let the project proceed because the River Ridge Development Authority, not the city, controls land use at the former Indiana Army Ammunition Plant site; Jeffersonville approves development plans there but has no zoning say. · source
  • 2026-08-04 — At the moratorium hearing, residents including Rachel Wurfel (living ~1.3 miles from the Meta site) and James McPherson (a retired IT/data-center engineer) raised construction-related foundation damage, light pollution, and heat/HVAC-cost concerns tied to the ~400 MW Meta facility; resident William Hunt argued the project's 2022 tax abatement was 'an extremely wrong deal.' · source
  • 2026-08-03 — The Jeffersonville City Council unanimously passed a one-year moratorium on new data center development (suspending normal rules to push it through three readings in one session), but the Meta facility already under construction at River Ridge is exempt due to federal/state control of the former Indiana Army Ammunition Plant site. · source
  • 2026-08-03 — The city disclosed that Meta paid $4 million upfront in wastewater capacity fees and is projected to generate $300,000-$400,000 in monthly recurring sewer utility revenue once the facility is operational. · source
  • 2025-10-07 — Meta said it had contributed nearly $500,000 to schools and nonprofits since its 2024 groundbreaking and announced a new Community Action Grants program for Clark County, amid community concerns over a Duke Energy 11% residential rate increase set to take effect in early 2026. · source
  • 2025-10-04 — Community groups including Hoosier Action and Clark County Moms held a meeting to organize opposition to the Meta data center, citing concerns the facility could use up to 5 million gallons of water daily and raise local utility costs. · source

Where the decision gets made

Jeffersonville City Council — Zoning ordinances, the Unified Development Ordinance, and the data-center moratorium for the City of Jeffersonville (Clark County) -- excludes River Ridge Commerce Center, which the River Ridge Development Authority separately controls.

Meets: 6:00 p.m. on the 1st and 3rd Mondays of each month (occasionally with a preceding special meeting, e.g. 5 p.m. before the regular 6 p.m. session).
Where: Room 101, City Hall, 500 Quartermaster Court, Jeffersonville, IN

Public comment: Public comment period at each regular meeting (residents spoke on the moratorium during the Aug. 4, 2026 meeting's comment section); meetings are livestreamed and archived on the city's Vimeo channel. General inquiries: (812) 285-6427.
