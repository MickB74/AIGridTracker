The project on the table

DataOne USA / Nebius Vineland (Microsoft) — Approved

Operator: Nebius · Owner: Nebius · Tenant: Microsoft · Filing LLC: NEP Real Estate of Vineland NJ Urban Renewal LLC; applicant changed to DataOne USA LLC in July 2025 · Size: 300 MW

South Jersey's largest data center: 2,647,628 sq ft approved in four phases over twelve years off South Lincoln and Sheridan avenues, built by DataOne USA for Nebius, whose GPU capacity here is contracted to Microsoft under a $17.4B five-year deal. Phase one was already operating before an environmental impact statement was published (Feb 2026). Three live disputes: NOISE - a county noise violation in March 2026 and a federal nuisance class action that was voluntarily dismissed on jurisdictional grounds in June 2026, not on the merits; POWER - the board approved 300 MW of behind-the-meter Bloom Energy fuel cells plus a 1.5-million-gallon LNG tank on Aug 17, 2026, after two city stop-work orders for building that equipment without permits; AIR - NJDEP says it has issued no permits and has no applications under review for power generators at the site, while thermal drone imagery published Aug 27, 2026 showed at least 45 of 62 gas generators running. Water figures conflict: the applicant's Aug 2026 case put annual use near 8 million gallons; a 2024 Vineland water department estimate cited by opponents put it above 1.1 billion gallons a year. Vineland has no data center moratorium; neighbouring Millville banned them outright in May 2026.

Next: Approved — pivot from stopping it to a binding CBA: enforceable caps on noise, water and cost, plus a community benefit.

Where the decision gets made

City Council — Zoning ordinances, redevelopment plans, city budgets and municipal loans -- including the $6.2 million Urban Enterprise Zone revolving-fund loan approved for the DataOne data center in December 2025. Council, not the Planning Board, is where a data center moratorium or zoning ban would have to pass; Vineland has not adopted one.

Meets: Regular meetings the 2nd and 4th Tuesday at 5:30 p.m.; work sessions the 1st and 3rd Tuesday at 5:00 p.m.
Where: City Council Chambers, 2nd floor of City Hall, 640 E. Wood Street, Vineland, NJ 08360

Public comment: Public comment is taken at the start of every regular meeting and work session. Three minutes per speaker, no transferring time, one approach to the podium per person per topic. The limit was cut from five minutes to three in March 2026 after months of data center opposition lengthened meetings.
