xAI Colossus — Approved

Operator: xAI · Tenant: xAI · Size: 2,000 MW

Operating and expanding (~$20B, ~2 GW ambition). The live fight is air quality: on-site gas turbines in southwest Memphis - an area already failing federal ozone standards - drew a Clean Air Act suit. Environmental groups' tracking put unpermitted turbines at 27 in Feb 2026, growing to 59 by mid-2026 across the Colossus complex (incl. Colossus 2 in Southaven, MS).

Next: Approved — pivot from stopping it to a binding CBA: enforceable caps on noise, water and cost, plus a community benefit.
