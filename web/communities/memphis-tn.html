The projects on the table

xAI Colossus — Approved

Operator: xAI · Tenant: xAI · Size: 2,000 MW

Operating and expanding (~$20B, ~2 GW ambition). The live fight is air quality: on-site gas turbines in southwest Memphis - an area already failing federal ozone standards - drew a Clean Air Act suit. Environmental groups' tracking put unpermitted turbines at 27 in Feb 2026, growing to 59 by mid-2026 across the Colossus complex (incl. Colossus 2 in Southaven, MS).

Next: Approved — pivot from stopping it to a binding CBA: enforceable caps on noise, water and cost, plus a community benefit.

Latest in the record

  • 2026-06-16 — The U.S. Department of Justice moved to intervene in the NAACP's Clean Air Act suit and urged the court to dismiss it, arguing that shutting off xAI's Southaven turbines would harm national, economic and energy security because the Grok AI model supports Department of War military operations, including recent strikes involving Iran. · source
  • 2026-05-06 — The NAACP asked the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Mississippi for a preliminary injunction to halt xAI's gas turbines at its Southaven facility while the Clean Air Act lawsuit proceeds, as reported May 6, 2026. · source
  • 2026-04-14 — NAACP, represented by Earthjustice and SELC, filed a Clean Air Act suit against xAI and MZX Tech (N.D. Miss.) over unpermitted gas turbines serving the Colossus complex. · source
  • 2026-03-10 — The Mississippi Environmental Quality Permit Board unanimously approved a PSD construction permit allowing xAI affiliate MZX Tech to install 41 permanent methane gas turbines at its Southaven plant powering the Memphis data center operations. · source
  • 2026-03-03 — xAI filed a permit for a $659.3 million, 75-foot, four-story building of roughly 312,000 square feet on a parcel adjacent to its Colossus 2 site on Tulane Road, reported March 3, 2026. · source
  • 2026-02-13 — Earthjustice and the Southern Environmental Law Center, on behalf of the national NAACP and its Mississippi State Conference, sent a 60-day notice of intent to sue xAI over 27 unpermitted gas turbines in Southaven powering the Colossus 2 data center. · source
