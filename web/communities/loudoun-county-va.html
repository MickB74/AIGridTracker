The data center fight in Loudoun County, Virginia

As of September 5, 2026, Loudoun County, Virginia has a data center moratorium under consideration (action taken Jul 22, 2026).

Status is derived, not stored — a pause with a documented end date flips to Expired on its own — and the source is right next to the claim.

Status today

Proposedterm not documentedstatus
Jul 22, 2026action taken
Locallevel

Board of Supervisors passed Supervisor Briskman's motion 6-1-2 (Umstattd opposed; Kershner and Letourneau absent) directing the county attorney to return in September with options for pausing new data center applications while the county finishes its data center standards. County Attorney Leo Rogers told the board a blanket moratorium is not legal in Virginia: legislative applications (rezonings, special exceptions) could be paused for up to a year, but administrative applications cannot because state code sets review clocks. The item is expected Sep 15, 2026. A separate proposal to strip the exemption from 23 grandfathered projects was deferred Sep 1 for legal review and returns Oct 6. World's largest data center hub

Amazon Bridgefield Tech Center (GW campus, Ashburn) — In review

Operator: Amazon (AWS) · Owner: Amazon Data Services · Tenant: Amazon (AWS) · Filing LLC: Amazon Data Services · Acres: 122

Four data center buildings (about 800,000 sq ft per WTOP; Northern Virginia Magazine reports ~400,000 sq ft plus three parking garages) on George Washington University's Virginia Science and Technology campus, filed as a site plan under 1972-era zoning so it would need no Planning Commission review, public hearing or Board vote. The county says data centers are not by-right there and will require special-exception review. Construction cannot start until GW vacates, up to five years. 'rezoning_filed' here is the site-plan filing date.

Next: Application filed. Ask the planning office for the hearing date and request formal notice.
