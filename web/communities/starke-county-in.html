The data center fight in Starke County, Indiana

As of September 11, 2026, Starke County, Indiana has a data center moratorium in force (action taken Dec 15, 2025; documented end date 2026-12-15).

Status is derived, not stored — a pause with a documented end date flips to Expired on its own — and the source is right next to the claim.

Board of Commissioners adopted Ordinance 2025-37 by a 3-0 vote on Dec 15, 2025, on the Plan Commission's Dec 4 favorable recommendation, barring building permits, improvement-location permits and site-plan applications for hyperscale data centers (>5,000 sq ft) in unincorporated Starke County for 12 months. Prompted by a Chicago-based firm's Nov 2025 inquiry about rezoning two parcels for a data center. The project on the table

Abei Energy data center proposal (rezoning request) — Blocked by ban

Operator: Abei Energy (data center division)

Chicago-based Abei Energy's data center division emailed the Starke County Plan Commission asking about rezoning two parcels from Agricultural to Industrial. County officials disclosed the inquiry to residents on 2025-11-03; Abei was placed on the Plan Commission's 2025-11-12 agenda. Exact parcels, acreage, and project size were never disclosed publicly. The county drafted a hyperscale data center moratorium ordinance in direct response and adopted it (Ordinance 2025-37) on 2025-12-15, before Abei's proposal advanced to a rezoning vote -- so this project's outcome is 'moratorium' (blocked by the county's own timeout), not a formal denial.

Next: Blocked by a local ban or moratorium - the project stopped at the ordinance, not at an application. Watch for the ordinance being challenged in court or allowed to lapse.
