The project on the table

Google Botetourt (Botetourt Center at Greenfield) — In review

Operator: Google · Owner: Google · Acres: 312

312 acres bought from the county for $14.06M (Jun 20, 2025) plus $4M in grants; performance agreement approved by the EDA Jun 20 and the Board of Supervisors Jun 24, 2025. First building due 2028; first-building cooling announced as air-cooled after a fight over drawing from Carvins Cove. A board-appointed water study commission (Jul 28, 2026, 3-1) reports within six months. Grading permit issued Aug 2026.

Next: An application is in the record. Ask the planning office for the next hearing date and request formal notice.

Latest in the record

  • 2026-11-23 — Substitute Judge Michael Doucette ruled the board violated the Virginia Freedom of Information Act at its June 24, 2025 meeting, rescinded all county actions on the project since then, enjoined further action under the performance agreement and ordered construction stopped. The county may hold a compliant public hearing and vote again; it said it is evaluating its options. · source
  • 2026-11-02 — County resident Robert Dallas petitioned Botetourt County Circuit Court to rescind the June 2025 performance agreement, alleging the Board of Supervisors violated the Virginia Freedom of Information Act by voting on it after a closed session without identifying it on the agenda or in open meeting. · source
  • 2026-08-28 — The county issued Google a Land Disturbance Permit allowing phased grading of the 312-acre site. · source
  • 2026-07-28 — Supervisors voted 3-1 to create a five-member, board-appointed commission — no Google or county affiliation — to study the data center's impact on regional water resources within six months. Supervisor Linda Rottman dissented, calling it 'a gigantic middle finger to everyone in the crowd.' · source
  • 2025-06-24 — Board of Supervisors approved the performance agreement: construction of the first data center to start in 2026 and the first building fit for occupancy in 2028, with a grant whenever Google's business personal property tax exceeds an effective $2.40 per $100. · source
  • 2025-06-20 — Google closed on 312 acres at Botetourt Center at Greenfield for $14,055,406 ($45,000/acre) and committed $4M in grants to county projects over five years; the Economic Development Authority approved the performance agreement the same day. · source

Where the decision gets made

Board of Supervisors — Zoning and land use; approved the Nov 2024 ordinance permitting data centers in the RAM district and the Jun 2025 Google performance agreement; created a five-member water study commission Jul 28, 2026 (3-1).

Meets: 4th Tuesday of each month, 2:00 p.m.
Where: Botetourt County Administration Center, 57 S Center Drive, Daleville, VA 24083

Public comment: Speak in person (3-minute limit), mail comments to 57 South Center Drive, Suite 200, Daleville, VA 24083, or use the online Citizen Comment Form; written comments must arrive by 9:00 a.m. on meeting day to enter the minutes. Livestream is view-only. Deputy Clerk Celeste Parker, 540-928-2006.
