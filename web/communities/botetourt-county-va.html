The project on the table

Google Botetourt (Botetourt Center at Greenfield) — Approved

Operator: Google · Owner: Google · Acres: 312

312 acres bought from the county for $14.06M (Jun 20, 2025) plus $4M in grants; performance agreement approved by the EDA Jun 20 and the Board of Supervisors Jun 24, 2025. First building due 2028; first-building cooling announced as air-cooled after a fight over drawing from Carvins Cove. A board-appointed water study commission (Jul 28, 2026, 3-1) reports within six months. Grading permit issued Aug 2026.

Next: Approved — pivot from stopping it to a binding CBA: enforceable caps on noise, water and cost, plus a community benefit.

Latest in the record

  • 2026-08-28 — The county issued Google a Land Disturbance Permit allowing phased grading of the 312-acre site. · source
  • 2026-07-28 — Supervisors voted 3-1 to create a five-member, board-appointed commission — no Google or county affiliation — to study the data center's impact on regional water resources within six months. Supervisor Linda Rottman dissented, calling it 'a gigantic middle finger to everyone in the crowd.' · source
  • 2025-06-24 — Board of Supervisors approved the performance agreement: construction of the first data center to start in 2026 and the first building fit for occupancy in 2028, with a grant whenever Google's business personal property tax exceeds an effective $2.40 per $100. · source
  • 2025-06-20 — Google closed on 312 acres at Botetourt Center at Greenfield for $14,055,406 ($45,000/acre) and committed $4M in grants to county projects over five years; the Economic Development Authority approved the performance agreement the same day. · source
  • 2024-11-26 — Board of Supervisors and Planning Commission, in a joint public hearing, unanimously approved a zoning ordinance text amendment defining data centers and permitting them in the Research and Advanced Manufacturing (RAM) district — the only zoning in the county that allows one. · source
