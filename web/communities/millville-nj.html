The project on the table

A1 Data Center (Millville) — Blocked by ban

Size: 1,400 MW · Acres: 66

A 2.9 million sq ft campus on 66 acres, with building heights to 150 feet, that would have drawn roughly 1.4 GW from the grid - the largest data center proposal ever blocked in New Jersey. No site plan was ever formally submitted to the city; the Board of Commissioners banned data centers citywide first, on May 19, 2026, calling them incompatible with Millville's land use objectives, infrastructure capacity and community character. Millville borders Vineland, where the 300 MW DataOne/Nebius campus was already under construction. Opposition was organised by the Climate Revolution Action Network; Mayor Dan Dixon described the ordinance as proactive rather than a response to a specific application.
