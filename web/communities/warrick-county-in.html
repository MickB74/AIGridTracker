The data center fight in Warrick County, Indiana

As of October 31, 2026, Warrick County, Indiana has a data center moratorium in force (action taken Aug 11, 2026; documented end date 2027-02-07).

The project on the table

Project Zora — In review

Acres: 300

Developer controls roughly 1,200 acres of reclaimed mine land in northern Warrick County; the preliminary concept would develop about 300 acres and leave 900 as buffer. County officials met the Project Zora development team on July 30, 2026, emphasizing no votes, commitments, or NDAs were signed and that the developer's capacity/cost claims are unverified. The end user is expected to be publicly identified by end of 2026, with no permit applications before January 2027; commissioners held an Aug 11, 2026 special meeting on data-center zoning-code amendments.

Next: An application is in the record. Ask the planning office for the next hearing date and request formal notice.

Latest in the record

  • 2026-08-11 — Warrick County Commissioners unanimously voted to enact a 180-day moratorium pausing all data center development, stating the pause would allow time for research and regulation development; residents at the meeting expressed concerns about water usage, noise, light pollution, and wildlife disruption, with some calling for a permanent ban rather than a temporary pause. · source
  • 2026-08-10 — At a Monday APC meeting, Warrick County residents pushed back against Project Zora as details emerged about the proposed data center on 1,200 acres of reclaimed mine land; commissioners confirmed they refused to sign a non-disclosure agreement with the developers and that no formal application or site review had been submitted. · source
  • 2026-07-31 — Warrick County Commissioners announced a special public meeting for August 11 at the Friedman Park Event Center in Newburgh to consider the APC's recommendation on amending the zoning code for data centers; commissioners disclosed the Project Zora developer expects to identify the end user by end of 2026 and will not submit permit applications before January 2027. · source
  • 2026-07-30 — Warrick County officials and Economic Development representatives met with the Project Zora development team; no votes, commitments, or NDAs signed. Developer described control of ~1,200 acres of reclaimed mine land with ~300 acres proposed for development. · source
  • 2026-07-15 — Wisdom 92.1 (WZDM) reported on data center opposition in Warrick County following the approximately 200-person APC hearing, noting residents' concerns about water use, noise, pollution, and loss of farmland, while proponents emphasized jobs and apprenticeship opportunities in the construction trades. · source
  • 2026-07-13 — Nearly 200 residents attended a 3.5-hour Warrick County APC public hearing on a proposed data center ordinance classifying facilities as small (under 10,000 sq ft), medium (10,001-40,000 sq ft), and large (over 40,000 sq ft), with large data centers not a permitted use; the board voted 5-2 to table the ordinance for four months. · source

Where the decision gets made

Warrick County Board of Commissioners — Zoning/land-use policy including data-center ordinances and moratoria; the Area Plan Commission (APC) and Board of Zoning Appeals (BZA) handle permanent zoning text and case-by-case variances/special exceptions, subject to Commissioner approval on ordinance matters.

Meets: Regular meetings on a recurring schedule; specific 2026 meeting dates are posted as a downloadable PDF on the county site rather than a fixed weekly/monthly cadence confirmable here.
Where: Historically held at the county administration building in Boonville, IN; the Aug 11 2026 special moratorium-vote meeting was held at the Friedman Park Event Center, 2700 Park Blvd, Boonville, IN, due to expected turnout.

Public comment: Not explicitly published on the Commissioners page as of this access; the page references an ADA Complaint Procedure document but no standing public-comment rule for regular meetings. The Area Plan Commission's rules of procedure (linked from warrickcounty.gov/area-plan) may govern comment periods for zoning hearings specifically.
