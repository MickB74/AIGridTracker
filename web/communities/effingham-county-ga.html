The project on the table

Project Camellia (OpenAI Effingham County) — In review

Operator: OpenAI · Owner: OpenAI · Tenant: OpenAI · Filing LLC: Octans GA, LLC · Size: 3,200 MW · Acres: 1,400

$20B OpenAI campus on 1,400 acres of the Savannah Gateway Industrial Hub, with 3.2 GW contracted from Georgia Power. The incentive deal was signed the day before the public learned of it.

Next: An application is in the record. Ask the planning office for the next hearing date and request formal notice.

Latest in the record

  • 2026-08-08 — An open-records investigation reconstructed how the deal moved from NDA to signed abatement without a public hearing, with residents learning the buyer's identity only after the incentives were locked in. · source
  • 2026-07-24 — Reporting confirmed OpenAI had contracted 3.2 gigawatts from Georgia Power for the site, delivered over roughly four years, despite public backlash over the secrecy of the deal. · source
  • 2026-07-22 — County leaders publicly announced the $20 billion, 1,400-acre campus one day after the tax agreement was signed, projecting up to 1,000 permanent jobs, an $80 million community investment fund, and revenue that could underwrite the county's plan to eliminate homestead property taxes. · source
  • 2026-07-21 — The Effingham County Industrial Development Authority signed a memorandum of understanding with Octans GA, LLC, an OpenAI affiliate, granting a 50% property tax abatement for 15 years locked to the 2026 millage rate. · source
  • 2026-02-02 — An OpenAI infrastructure official circulated a mutual non-disclosure agreement to Effingham County officials, asking them to sign before receiving confidential briefings on the project — the start of negotiations conducted outside public view. · source

Where the decision gets made

Board of Commissioners — Zoning, land use, and county budget across five districts plus an at-large chairman.
