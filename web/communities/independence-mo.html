The project on the table

Nebius Independence campus — Approved

Operator: Nebius · Owner: Nebius · Size: 800 MW · Acres: 398

Nebius' first gigawatt-scale US campus: ~2.5M sq ft on 398 acres near Route 78 and Little Blue Parkway, powered by a privately financed plant at the retired Blue Valley Power Plant site. Council approved Chapter 100 abatement 5-2 and the company has broken ground.

Next: Approved — pivot from stopping it to a binding CBA: enforceable caps on noise, water and cost, plus a community benefit.
