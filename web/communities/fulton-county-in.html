The data center fight in Fulton County, Indiana

As of September 11, 2026, Fulton County, Indiana has a data center moratorium in force (action taken Mar 2, 2026; documented end date 2027-03-02).

The project on the table

Decennial Group Akron Data Center Campus (proposed) — Blocked by ban

Operator: Decennial Group · Size: 500 MW · Acres: 300

Decennial Group co-founder David Pavlick (also spelled 'Pavlik' in some coverage) proposed a roughly 500 MW, 300-acre hyperscale data center campus near Akron, in Fulton County, no specific parcel/site was ever named publicly, and no hyperscale tenant was named (Pavlick said an unnamed hyperscale company larger than Decennial's existing CoreWeave-tenanted Hammond, IN facility was interested). Estimated $100-150 million in associated infrastructure investment (roads, bridges, community water upgrades) plus a closed-loop water design were described at a Feb. 4, 2026 public open house at the Akron Banquet Hall attended by 40+ residents; reporting says commissioners themselves learned of the proposal only about 10 days before that open house. The Fulton County Area Plan Commission recommended a 12-month moratorium 6-1 on Feb. 10, 2026 specifically in response to this proposal, and the Board of Commissioners adopted it 2-1 on March 2, 2026, effectively pausing the project. No later announcement of the project being formally withdrawn was located; it is currently blocked by the moratorium rather than confirmed dead.

Next: Blocked by a local ban or moratorium - the project stopped at the ordinance, not at an application. Watch for the ordinance being challenged in court or allowed to lapse.

Where the decision gets made

Fulton County Board of Commissioners (moratorium vote); Fulton County Area Plan Commission (recommending body / oversees the data center review committee) — Board of Commissioners adopted the moratorium ordinance 2-1 on March 2, 2026; the Area Plan Commission recommended it 6-1 on Feb. 10, 2026 and appoints the data center review committee tasked with drafting the post-moratorium ordinance.

Meets: Board of Commissioners meets as posted; Area Plan Commission meets the 4th Monday of every month unless otherwise posted.
Where: Fulton County Office Building, Commissioner's Room, 125 E. 9th Street, Rochester, IN 46975

Public comment: Public comment taken at posted Board of Commissioners and Area Plan Commission meetings; agendas and packets posted in advance via the county's Agenda Center.
